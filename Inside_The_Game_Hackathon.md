## Hackathon Home Page

Microsoft and the Premier League invite developers worldwide to go beyond the scoreline and reimagine how football can be understood, experienced, and shared.

Build an AI-powered solution that transforms synthetic, football-realistic data into explainable match intelligence, real-time narratives, automated recaps, and personalized experiences for fans, studios, broadcasters, and streaming platforms.

Football is more than a final score. Every pass, shot, tackle, change in possession, and shift in pressure can reveal a deeper story about the match.

Inside the Game: Developer Hackathon challenges you to build an AI-driven application or agent that turns synthetic football events into useful, understandable, and engaging match intelligence.

**Important data requirement**

*Projects must use synthetic, football-realistic data. No real Premier League match data is redistributed to participants.*

### What to Submit

Your submission must include:

- A newly built Project that meets the project requirements.
- A Project Pitch or Description: a short elevator pitch for your Project including what you built, which Microsoft and Azure technologies used, what it does, and what problem it solves.
- A demonstration video of your Project. The video portion of the Submission must:
  - Be less than two (2) minutes in length. Judges are not required to watch beyond two (2) minutes;
  - Include footage that shows the Project functioning on the device and platform for which it was built;
  - Provide a public URL to your demo video on YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook Video, or Youku, on the project page on the Hackathon Website; and
  - Not include third party trademarks, or copyrighted music or other material unless the Entrant has permission to use such material.
- A URL to your GitHub code repository. Your repository must be public for your entry to be considered.

### Judging

Projects are evaluated by experts and product teams using this rubric:

1. **Technological Implementation (20%)**
   - Quality software development practices
   - Effective use of hero technologies
   - Well-structured, documented, and maintainable code
2. **Agentic Design & Innovation (20%)**
   - Creative application of Agentic AI patterns
   - Sophisticated agent orchestration or multi-agent collaboration
   - Novel AI implementation or meaningful improvements
3. **Real-World Impact & Applicability (20%)**
   - Significance of the problem being solved
   - Production deployment readiness
   - Potential impact on developers, businesses, or end users
4. **User Experience & Presentation (20%)**
   - Well-designed and intuitive user experience
   - Clear communication of project value in demo
   - Balanced frontend and backend implementation
5. **Adherence to Hackathon Category (20%)**
   - Comply with the description of the applicable hackathon category.

## Challenge

### The Synthetic Match Insights Engine for Premier League Studio

An AI insights engine runs on Azure that explains the game beyond the stats, turning football events into real-time insights, key narratives and recaps, personalized for every fan, for studio and streaming use.

#### Description

Football stories do not start and end with the scoreline. In this hackathon, you will build AI-driven applications and agents that help transform synthetic football events into explainable match intelligence. Projects should help surface meaningful stats, identify key moments, explain why they matter, and generate personalized experiences. Projects must use synthetic, football-realistic data. No real Premier League match data is redistributed to participants. Participants should deliver an AI-powered solution that transforms synthetic football events into explainable match intelligence, real-time narratives, and automated match recaps suitable for studio, broadcast, or streaming scenarios, and flexible enough for fans in any market to tailor their experience.

Your Project may take the form of an AI agent, workflow, integration, data platform, analytics experience, automation solution, or an entirely new experience. Your Project should fit seamlessly into the intended use cases, be responsive enough for live play, clear enough for real-time narratives, and automated match recaps suitable for studio, broadcast, or streaming scenarios and flexible enough for fans in any market to tailor their experience.

**(i) One pipeline with five stages.** Your Project should:

- **(a) Ingest** synthetic match events as they happen (passes, shots, tackles, possession changes, pressure events, match metadata, etc.).
- **(b) Interpret** them into meaningful football statistics, patterns, and the context behind them.
- **(c) Explain** why a moment matters rather than only reporting that it happened.
- **(d) Render** that insight into something that can sit on screen alongside the match.
- **(e) Personalize it** so the same intelligence reaches a data-hungry analyst and a casual fan as two genuinely different experiences. Your Project should make every match feel made for every fan, including customized modes, favorite club(s), favorite player(s), player focused mode to focus on the player throughout the match and welcome many more ideas. Your Project might focus on on-screen storytelling, clean outputs, timed and machine-readable enough to be rendered as synchronized graphical overlays on a live match feed, in the way the platform's rendering partner handles overlays in the production path. Or your Project might focus on personalized audience experiences, viewing modes shaped by language preference, favorite club, favorite player, or a single performance metric a viewer cares about. Or it could do both, a Project that only generates metrics has no audience, and a solution that only personalizes has nothing to personalize.

**(ii) Real-time match intelligence:** ingest synthetic match events and aggregate them into meaningful football states and statistics. Features you should consider incorporating into your Project include:

- **Player identification:** real-time player name tagging, with optional speed and distance indicators triggered when predefined thresholds are reached
- **Pass quality:** pass distance, pass accuracy and a pass difficulty rating
- **Speed metrics:** ball speed and shot speed
- **Auto-eventing:** running a model over an incoming video feed to generate the pass and speed metrics above automatically, rather than tagging them by hand

**(iii) Narrative generation:** go beyond metrics to produce key narratives, player-performance context, notable milestones, and match recaps.

**(iv) Explainability:** explain why moments matter, such as control vs. chaos, tactical pressure changes, and game rhythm patterns.

**(v) Data innovation:** create or extend synthetic football-realistic datasets with events such as passes, shots, tackles, possession changes, pressure events, and match metadata.

**(vi) Multi-language storytelling:** generate clear natural-language outputs that could support global studio and streaming scenarios in multiple languages.
